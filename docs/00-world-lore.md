## Background

- A **meteorite bound to the Immaterium** falls in the south of the continent.
- **Elves and Dwarves** fight **Humans and Orcs** for its energy, up to the **Great Battle at the gates of the desert**.
- The arcane energy makes the asteroid react: **magic goes out**, and a rift towards the Immaterium releases **extraplanar creatures**.
- The **Army of the World's End** seals the portal with a **runic ritual**, but the region around the crater stays devastated: the **Southern Wastelands** are born.
- With magic unstable, civilisations switch to **steam**. Discharged veterans soup up old military vehicles, and the clandestine races begin: the **Steam Races Underground**.
- **One hundred years later** steam rules, peace is fragile, and the heart of the races is **Iron City**, the neutral city of the **Steam Council**.

## Implications for art and design

| Lore element | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Four factions (Elves, Dwarves, Humans, Orcs) | Four vehicle design languages and biomes (doc 01 §1.2–1.3). Orc designs must stay original (IP note, doc 01 §1.5). |
| Old military vehicles, souped up | Vehicles are war machines repurposed: tanks, half-tracks, haulers with welded-on parts. |
| Magic is unstable, runes still sealing the rift | "Rune-tech" glow (doc 02 emissive presets) is rare, precious, sometimes flickering. |
| Southern Wastelands around the crater | Desert/scrap biomes, crater landmarks, extraplanar remains as set dressing. |
| Iron City, neutral, Steam Council | Hub/menu setting; the foundry biome (D6) belongs to Iron City. |
| Underground races | Crowds, betting, sponsors, makeshift barricades (gameplay.md §6.3). |
| Origin: the board game "Steam Races" | Strategy over pure driving skill: gears, boiler, cards, grit (gameplay.md). Assets and the track editor should also serve tabletop tiles/miniatures (doc 03 §7.3). |
